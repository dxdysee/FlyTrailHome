YOUR DATA, YOUR RUN

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 23 October 2026

FlyTrail turns a GPX running route into a video. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information you provide

GPX routes

When you choose a GPX file, FlyTrail reads its route points and any available timestamps, elevation, and speed data to calculate run statistics and create the map video. The app processes the selected route on your device. FlyTrail does not upload the GPX file to a FlyTrail server.

GPX files can contain precise location history and timestamps. Only choose a file you are comfortable using in FlyTrail. The route may be visible in the video you create.

Videos

Videos you create are stored in FlyTrail's app storage on your device so you can preview, share, or delete them later. They remain there until you delete them or remove the app. When you choose to share a video, send it by SMS, or save it to Files, iOS presents the relevant system interface and the destination you choose receives the content.

Apple services and subscriptions

FlyTrail uses Apple MapKit to display map imagery. Map requests are handled by Apple and may reveal the map area being displayed to Apple. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

If you purchase the FlyTrail subscription, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. FlyTrail receives subscription entitlement information from StoreKit to determine whether subscription features are available. FlyTrail does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy and applicable App Store terms.

Information FlyTrail does not collect

Based on the current app implementation, FlyTrail does not require an account, request device location access, run advertising or analytics SDKs, or send GPX files or saved videos to a FlyTrail-operated server. FlyTrail uses route coordinates from the GPX file you select; it does not obtain the device's live location.

Retention and deletion

Saved videos remain in the app's local storage until you delete them in FlyTrail or remove the app. You can contact the publisher using the email below with privacy questions or requests related to information handled by FlyTrail. App Store purchase records are handled by Apple.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be available at the public privacy policy URL listed on the FlyTrail App Store page.
